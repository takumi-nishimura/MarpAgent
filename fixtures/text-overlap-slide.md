## Remote work support

- Employment of people with disabilities keeps rising
  - About 705,000 people, up 4.0% year on year<sup>[1]</sup>
- Policy focus moves from quantity to quality
  - Promote the use of individual abilities<sup>[2]</sup>
- Avatar robots enable remote customer service
  - Most tasks are still routine
- Remote workers want to grow in their roles
  - 38.1% prefer growth-oriented work<sup>[3]</sup>
  - Field trials show pairs share tasks in different ways<sup>[4]</sup>

<div style="margin-top: 4.6em; text-align: center"><strong>Work that lets people express themselves<br>and see their contribution is needed</strong></div>
